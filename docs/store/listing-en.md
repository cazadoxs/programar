### Full description (max 4000)

```text
Instagram, TikTok and YouTube are designed so you can't put them down. Foco is designed so you can. 🔦

Foco is an app blocker for studying, working and beating procrastination. You don't have to delete anything: you choose what distracts you, when, and for how long. Block only Reels and keep your messages. Block TikTok completely during exam season. And when you're tempted to switch it off, Foco adds a little friction so you think twice.

🎯 BLOCK ONLY WHAT STEALS YOUR TIME
• Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Snapchat, Facebook, X, Reddit and WhatsApp.
• The whole app or just parts of it: Reels and Shorts, Stories, Explore, Messages or the infinite feed.
• Example: on Instagram, block Reels and Explore but keep chatting with your friends.
• Daily limits: 20 minutes of YouTube a day, and when it's gone, it's gone.

⏱️ POMODORO AND STUDY SESSIONS
• Pomodoro timer (25/5 or your own rhythm): blocks only during focus time.
• Study or work sessions with a goal: "Study chapter 4", 90 minutes.
• Automatic schedules: Monday to Friday 9:00–14:00, or all day.
• Friction before stopping: wait a few seconds and type a phrase to unlock.
• Strict mode (Pro): once you start, there's no way back.

📅 ALL YOUR CALENDARS IN ONE
• Google Calendar and Outlook / Microsoft 365.
• Samsung Calendar, Xiaomi and any calendar on your phone.
• iCloud and university timetables via .ics link.
• Tasks and events together in your daily agenda.

🤖 AI ASSISTANT (WITH YOUR OWN KEY)
• Connect your Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (ChatGPT) or Google (Gemini) API key.
• "Plan my day": spreads your tasks into focus blocks.
• Moves events, finds free slots and reschedules what you didn't get done.
• The AI asks you to confirm its changes, and everything can be undone.

⚡ QUICK CAPTURE
• Double-press volume down to jot a task on the fly, even inside another app.
• Or dictate it: Foco transcribes it and understands "dentist tomorrow at 5".

💻 PHONE AND COMPUTER
• One account that syncs your Android phone and the web version for your computer.

🔒 PRIVACY
• Which apps you use and what's on your screen is analysed only on your phone: it never leaves it.
• Your AI keys are stored encrypted and only used to talk to the provider you choose.
• No ads. We don't sell your data.

💛 FREE, WITH A ONE-TIME FOCO PRO
Foco is free forever: block parts of up to 2 apps, 3 schedules, 1 cloud calendar and 7-day stats.
Foco Pro is a one-time, lifetime purchase, no subscription: unlimited apps, rules and calendars, strict mode, full stats history, AI "work mode" presets, themes, and you support an independent developer.

Great for exam prep, studying for civil-service exams, working from home without distractions, or simply to stop scrolling before bed.

ℹ️ ACCESSIBILITY SERVICE USE
With your explicit permission, Foco uses the Android Accessibility API only to detect which app or section (for example, Reels) is on screen and block the ones you chose, and to detect the double-press of the volume button for quick capture. Foco does not collect, store or share what you type or the content of your apps. If you don't enable accessibility, Foco can still block whole apps using usage access.

Made in Valencia, Spain, by a student, for anyone who wants their time back. Ideas or issues? Write to me: [email]
```
